DoseLift Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

DoseLift: GLP-1 Health Tracker ("DoseLift", "the app") is published by Kyle Roth. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, and your choices. We do not sell your data, we show no ads, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data that stays on your device

Your profile, medication and dose log, weigh-ins, workouts, food log, goals and settings are stored on your device. We do not have a copy of them on our servers.

Apple Health (optional)

If you allow it, DoseLift reads steps, active energy, weight and workouts from Apple Health, and writes your weigh-ins and the calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber you log. You choose what to share and can change this at any time in Settings > Health. Health data is not sent to our servers or used for advertising.

Features that use our servers

These features send limited information off your device to work:

  • Food search and barcode scan. The text you search for or the barcode number is sent to our backend, which looks it up in public food databases (USDA FoodData Central, FatSecret and Open Food Facts).
  • AI meal photo and AI meal ideas (optional). Only if you say yes on the consent screen, the meal photo, or your daily calorie and protein targets and food preferences, are sent to our backend and on to a third-party AI provider to produce the estimate or suggestions. You can turn these off at any time in Settings. We do not use this data to train AI models or for advertising.

Requests are tied to a random anonymous ID created by the app, not to your name or email. We keep basic usage records (the anonymous ID and request counts) for up to 90 days to prevent abuse and enforce limits. Your anonymous ID is shown in Settings.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription status, basic device and app information, and paywall events such as which paywall screen was shown. It does not receive your name, email, weight, dose, workout or food data. Apple processes your payment; we never see your card details.

Camera and notifications

The camera is used only to photograph meals, read nutrition labels and scan barcodes. Label reading happens on your device. Notifications (dose and weigh-in reminders and a few first-week tips) are scheduled locally on your device.

Data retention and deletion

Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. To ask us to delete server-side usage records tied to your anonymous ID, contact us with that ID.

Children

DoseLift is intended for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Not medical advice

DoseLift is a tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice or recommend or calculate doses. Talk to your prescriber about your medication.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Questions or requests: doselift@gmail.com.
