DoseLift Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

DoseLift: GLP-1 Health Tracker ("DoseLift", "the app") is published by Kyle Roth. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, and your choices. DoseLift has no accounts, no ads, no analytics or crash-reporting tools, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites. We do not sell your data.

Data that stays on your device

Your profile (such as name or nickname, sex, birthday, height and weight), medication name, dose and dose log, weigh-ins, workouts, food log, goals, reminder settings and preferences are stored on your device. We do not keep a copy of them on our servers. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Apple Health (optional)

If you allow it, DoseLift reads date of birth, biological sex, height, weight, step count, active energy and workouts from Apple Health, and writes your weigh-ins and the calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber you log. You choose each data type in the iOS permission sheet and can change it any time in Settings. Apple Health data is used on your device and is not sent to our servers.

Features that use our servers

These features send limited information off your device so they can work:

  • Food search and barcode scan. The text you search for or the barcode number is sent to our server, which looks it up in public food databases (USDA FoodData Central, FatSecret and Open Food Facts). Those services see our server, not your device. Results are kept in a shared cache that is not tied to you and is deleted automatically after a limited time (between 23 hours and 30 days, depending on the type of result).
  • AI meal photo and AI meal ideas (optional, off until you allow it). Only after you allow AI in Settings, a meal photo (resized, with location and camera details removed) or your remaining calorie and macro targets, meal type, diet style, meals per day, eating preferences and foods to avoid are sent through our server to Anthropic, our AI provider, to produce the estimate or suggestions. Your name, weight, medication, dose, workouts, Health data and anonymous ID are not sent. Our server does not store the photo or the request. Anthropic handles them under its own terms. You can turn AI off any time in Settings > Privacy.

These requests use a random anonymous ID created by the app, not your name or email. We keep per-day counts of photo analyses, searches and meal plans against that ID to enforce daily limits. They are kept until you ask us to delete your anonymous account. Our hosting provider (Supabase) may also see your IP address when a request arrives. Your anonymous ID is shown in Settings > Privacy.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives purchase and renewal information, receipts, its own random anonymous app user ID, the app version, and device and operating system details needed to validate a purchase, plus paywall events such as which screen was shown. We do not pass it your name, email, or any health, dose, weight, workout or food data. Apple processes your payment, and we never see your card details.

Camera and notifications

The camera is used only to photograph meals, read nutrition labels and scan barcodes. Nutrition labels are read on your device, and the label image is not uploaded. Notifications (dose and weigh-in reminders and a few first-week tips) are scheduled locally on your device.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. To have us delete your anonymous account and its usage counts from our server, email us the anonymous ID shown in Settings > Privacy. We will delete it within 30 days. Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies.

Children

DoseLift is intended for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Not medical advice

DoseLift is a tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice or recommend or calculate doses. Talk to your prescriber about your medication.

Changes and contact
