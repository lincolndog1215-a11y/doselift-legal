Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose DoseLift, and tap Cancel Subscription. If you are in a free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends to avoid being charged.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?
Open DoseLift, go to the subscription screen, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the DoseLift purchase.

How do I delete my data?
Your profile, doses, weights, workouts and food log are stored on your device and are deleted when you delete the app. To ask us to delete the small amount of data on our servers (usage counts tied to your anonymous account), email us and include the anonymous ID shown in the app's Settings if it is available.

The food photo or meal ideas feature is not working.
These features need an internet connection and have a daily limit. Try again in a few minutes, and email us if it keeps happening.

Does DoseLift give medical advice?
No. DoseLift is a tracking tool. It does not recommend or calculate medication doses. Always follow your doctor's instructions.
